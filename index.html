Privacy Policy

In short

  • Your inspection data is stored on your phone. If you sign in and join a company workspace, it is also stored in the cloud in Sydney, Australia so your team can share it.
  • Voice notes are sent to OpenAI to turn speech into text. The audio is not kept.
  • When a report is created in English, item descriptions and room names written in other languages are sent to OpenAI to be translated. Only that text is sent — no photos, names or addresses.
  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and your data is never sold.
  • You can delete your account and your company's cloud data at any time from inside the app.

Who we are

DefectWalk is a site inspection app made in Australia by its independent developer ("we", "us"). We handle personal information in line with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles. Contact us about privacy at support@khinvestgroup.com.au.

How we use information

Only to provide and support DefectWalk: storing and syncing your inspections, transcribing voice notes, translating report text, producing PDF / Excel reports, signing you in, managing your company workspace and subscription, preventing abuse, and answering your feedback. We do not use your inspection content for marketing and we do not sell or rent personal information.

Storage, retention and deletion

  • On your device — kept until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app.
  • In the cloud — kept while your company workspace exists and its subscription is active. Deleted items are removed from members' devices on their next sync.
  • If your subscription ends — the app becomes read-only: you can still view everything and export PDF / Excel reports and a full backup file (all projects, items and photos). We remind you in the app and by email to export a backup when the subscription stops renewing. Cloud data and photos are kept for 6 months after the subscription ends; if you resubscribe within that time everything is restored. After 6 months the workspace's cloud projects and photos are permanently deleted. Data on your devices is not affected.
  • Voice recordings — deleted from your device after transcription and never saved on our servers.
  • Deleting your account — Account tab → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account; if you own a company workspace, the workspace and all of its cloud projects and photos are deleted for every member. Encrypted backups kept by our hosting provider are overwritten within about 30 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and at rest by our hosting provider. Each company's data is isolated by database access rules so only its members can read or change it. Sign-in uses one-time email codes, so there is no password to steal. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export (PDF, Excel or a full backup file) and delete your information directly in the app. You can also ask us for access to or correction of personal information we hold by emailing support@khinvestgroup.com.au; we will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with how we handle a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au).

Support

Contact us

The quickest way to reach us is Settings → Feedback in the app: describe the problem or idea and, if you'd like a reply, add your email. You can also email support@khinvestgroup.com.au. We usually reply within two business days.

Common questions

  • Do I need an account? No. On your own (Solo) you can record inspections and export reports without signing in. Sign in on the Account tab only to work as a team.
  • How do I join my company? Ask your admin for an invite code, then Account tab → sign in with your email → Join your company → enter the code. Don't tap "Create a new company" — that makes a separate, empty company.
  • The sign-in code didn't arrive. Check your junk / spam folder, wait a minute, then tap “Use a different email / resend” to send a new code.
  • Voice notes don't work. Voice notes need microphone permission (iPhone Settings → DefectWalk) and an internet connection. You can always type instead.
  • How do I make a report? Open a project, folder or unit and tap Create inspection report. Choose locations and filters, then export PDF or Excel or email it.
  • How do I back up or move to a new phone? Settings → Export backup creates a file with all projects, items and photos; use Import on the new phone. Team workspaces also sync automatically when you sign in.
  • How do I delete my account? Account tab → Delete account.
